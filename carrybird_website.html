For customers

Getting what you need, made simple.

  1. Step 1

    Request what you need

    Describe the item you want and your preferred local shop. Add the quantity and delivery location.

  2. Step 2

    Compare delivery bids

    Eligible Birds can offer delivery charges. Compare the available offers and choose the one that suits you.

  3. Step 3

    Get it delivered

    Once the price and request are confirmed, your Bird purchases the item and delivers it to you.

Why CarryBird

Flexible local shopping, not another catalogue.

Where we fit

CarryBird focuses on flexible local shopping rather than competing solely on instant grocery delivery. We do not claim to be cheaper or faster than quick-commerce apps.

  • Neighbourhood shops

    Access to products from participating neighbourhood shops.

  • Beyond a fixed catalogue

    Flexibility to request items beyond a fixed online catalogue.

  • Extra earnings for Birds

    An opportunity for people to earn extra money while fulfilling suitable requests.

Trust and transparency

Three prices, kept apart.

What you pay is always the sum of three separate parts, so you can see which is which.

Item costWhat the item costs at the shop. An estimate until the shop price is confirmed.
+
Bird delivery bidThe delivery charge a Bird offers. Chosen from eligible offers.
+
CarryBird feeThe platform fee, shown on its own line.
=
TotalExample: ₹100 + ₹20 + ₹1 = ₹121

Bids are for delivery only

Birds bid on the delivery charge. The item price is never part of the bid, so a bid cannot raise what the item costs.

Estimate, then confirmed

An item price entered with a request is an estimate. It is distinguished from the confirmed shop price.

Examples are examples

Every rupee figure on this page is illustrative. It is not a live price, a local price list or a promised fee.

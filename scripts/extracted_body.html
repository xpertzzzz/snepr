Help

Good to
know.

Answers marked TBC need your real product rules before launch.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Customers can use Snepr in a browser. A native app can be offered when available.

How do I join a live queue?

Choose a salon and service, then join the digital queue where that salon has enabled queue joining.

How are wait times calculated?

Wait estimates should use the salon's active chairs, queue position and service duration. Actual wait can change as the salon operates.

Can I cancel my token?

Cancellation rules depend on the salon's configured queue policy. Confirm the final policy before launch.

How do I redeem Snepr Points?

Eligible points can be applied as a service discount under the proposed redemption rules. Final redemption flow is subject to backend support.
